# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Senior leaders and marketing executives (CMO, VP, CEO level) at mid-to-large organizations across North America. They arrive via LinkedIn, a referred email, or after seeing Conner speak. Context: evaluating whether to book a keynote, engage a coaching retainer, or subscribe to The Brief. Decision is trust-based; they're buying access to someone's judgment, not a SaaS product.

## Product Purpose

A personal brand site for Conner Galway — AI coach, consultant, and speaker. Exists to convert warm leads into booked calls or newsletter subscribers. Success means: a visitor who arrives with mild curiosity leaves having booked a discovery call, subscribed to The Brief, or at minimum, confident enough to return.

## Brand Personality

Confident without being loud. Sharp without being cold. Practitioner, not preacher.

## Anti-references

- Generic "consultant template" sites (navy/cream/stock photo of handshake)
- Dark minimal SaaS aesthetic (Vercel-style) — wrong register
- Editorial magazine cliché (display serif + italic + broadsheet grid) — too studied
- Warm-earthy AI default (forest green on cream) — what was just replaced
- Motivational-speaker hype (oversized gradient text, stock confetti)

## Design Principles

1. **Earned authority, not announced authority.** Copy and layout show mastery; they don't claim it. A visitor should feel Conner's credibility before they read a testimonial.
2. **One thing per fold.** Each viewport has a single dominant idea. No competing messages. Long scroll, deliberate pacing.
3. **Typography does the heavy lifting.** The visual system is typographic first. Color and layout support the hierarchy; they don't replace it.
4. **Honest specificity.** Every word earns its place. Real names, real companies, real outcomes. No buzzwords, no vague transformation language.
5. **Invite, don't pressure.** The CTA is clear and repeated, but the rhythm of the page earns the ask rather than forcing it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. Large headings ≥ 3:1. All animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Focus states visible. Semantic HTML throughout.
